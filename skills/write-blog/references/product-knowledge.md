## Subscription Plans

<!-- Pricing guidance for blog content -->

Do not include specific prices in blog posts (they change). Instead:
- Reference plan tier names
- Link to your pricing page for current pricing
- Mention "free plan available" or "starts free" when relevant

## Brand Voice in Blogs

- Refer to the product as "[Product Name]" (specify exact casing)
- No superlatives about your own product ("the best", "the most powerful")
- Let features speak for themselves with specific examples
- Acknowledge competitor strengths honestly -- readers trust balanced takes
- Position your product as "one great option" not "the only option"
